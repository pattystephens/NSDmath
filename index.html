Want to Create Your Own?

Every activity here started as a prompt. You can build your own for your grade level and standard in four steps:

  1. Open the NSD Prompt Libraries and choose the library listed below.
  2. Fill in your grade level, standard, topic and curriculum.
  3. Copy the prompt and paste it into Claude.
  4. Review what Claude makes, then ask for any changes before using it with students.
